Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Envora Flexi Private Limited

1. Introduction & Overview

Envora Flexi Private Limited ("Envora Flexi", "we", "us", or "our") manufactures and supplies Flexible Stone Sheets (MCM – Modified Clay Material) for architectural interior and exterior cladding applications. We respect the privacy of architects, interior designers, contractors, developers, trade partners, and clients who interact with our website, request material samples, or inquire about our collections.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, safeguard, and use personal information gathered through our website, WhatsApp concierge communication, email correspondences, and physical sample distribution channels.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill trade inquiries, dispatch physical material sample boxes, provide commercial quotations, and offer technical assistance:

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional title, architecture studio or firm name, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, and official email address.
  • Sample & Delivery Details: Physical courier delivery address, pin/postal code, city, and state for sample box logistics.
  • Project Specifications: Selected stone finish (Marble Finish, Marble Print, Textured Stone, Natural Stone-Look), chosen thickness profile (3mm, 5mm, or 8mm), estimated square meterage/footage, substrate conditions, and curved wall application requirements.
  • Commercial Communications: Records of inquiries submitted via our on-site drawer form, email correspondence sent to envoraflexi@gmail.com, and WhatsApp chat transcripts with our concierge.

B2B Trade Guarantee: Envora Flexi operates solely as a manufacturing and supply partner. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade client or architectural project information to any marketing aggregators or third-party advertisers.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate commercial and trade fulfillment purposes, including:

  • Dispatching curated Flexible Stone sample swatches and physical specification kits to your studio or site address.
  • Calculating technical square-meter material requirements, freight logistics, and commercial quotations.
  • Providing engineering advisory on minimum cold-bending radii ($R \ge 200\text{ mm}$), recommended adhesives, and substrate preparation.
  • Maintaining commercial invoices, shipping manifests, and compliance records required by Indian corporate and tax legislation.

5. Disclosure of Data to Logistics Carriers

To deliver physical material samples and order shipments, your recipient name, address, and contact number are shared strictly with verified courier and logistics providers (e.g., DTDC, Blue Dart, Delhivery, or dedicated freight handlers). These carriers are granted access solely to complete physical parcel delivery.

6. Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard organizational and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or loss of client data. Inquiries are stored on secure corporate servers accessible only to authorized sales and engineering personnel bound by strict confidentiality.

7. Data Retention

We retain client contact and specification data only for the duration necessary to manage your ongoing architectural project, fulfill warranty and sample obligations, and comply with statutory legal and financial record-keeping requirements under Indian law.

8. Your Rights & Options

You retain the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal and project records we retain for your account.
  • Right to Correction: Request updates or corrections to your delivery address, studio details, or contact phone.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your contact records from our active trade database, subject to statutory tax and invoice retention laws.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our grievance desk at envoraflexi@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Subject Request".

9. Cookies & Analytical Tracking

Our website utilizes a clean, privacy-conscious architecture. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site trackers or ad-retargeting pixels. Any session cookies or local browser storage used are strictly functional—such as remembering active sample drawer choices or preferences.
