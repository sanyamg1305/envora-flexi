Why Flexible Stone

12 structural and architectural advantages of Modified Clay Material (MCM) over traditional stone cladding.

01 Structural Load

Light on the structure

  • Lightweight compared to natural stone
  • Reduces structural load on buildings
  • Easy to transport and handle
02 Curvature & Form

Free to design

  • Flexible and bendable material
  • Suits curved and uneven surfaces
  • Available in various colours, textures and designs
03 Site Efficiency

Faster, cleaner installation

  • Easy and faster installation
  • Less labour and installation cost
  • Less dust and pollution during application
04 Application Range

Premium and versatile

  • Modern and premium stone-like finishes
  • Suitable for interior and exterior applications
  • An alternative to traditional stone and heavy cladding materials

Three thicknesses

Thinner sheets bend more easily. Thicker sheets are stiffer and suit larger flat areas.

3mm

For curves

The most flexible. Columns, rounded corners, arches and feature walls.

5mm

All-round

A balance of flexibility and stiffness for interior walls and gentle curves.

8mm

For large surfaces

The sturdiest. Large flat areas, heavy-wear zones and exterior facades.

Applications

  • Interior walls

    Living areas, lobbies, bathrooms and feature walls.

  • Exterior facades

    Lightweight, weather-resistant cladding for building exteriors.

  • Curved walls and columns

    Bends around curved architectural surfaces where rigid stone cannot.

  • Uneven surfaces

    Follows irregular substrates directly during installation.

  • Renovation

    Tile-over-tile application with less load, zero dust and lower labour.

Installation & FAQs

Clear, direct answers for architects, interior designers, and installation contractors.

Flexible polymer tile adhesive (C2TE S1/S2 grade) is recommended for cement and masonry substrates. For drywall, plywood, or tile-over-tile renovation, use high-strength MS polymer mastic or polyurethane structural adhesive with 100% troweled coverage.
Sheets cut cleanly with a standard heavy-duty utility knife and straightedge, or mechanical shears. There is no need for wet diamond grinders, and zero hazardous airborne crystalline silica dust is produced on site.
Yes. Clean the existing ceramic tile surface, apply a bonding primer, and bond Envora Flexi sheets with MS polymer adhesive. This cuts renovation downtime from weeks to hours with zero demolition mess.
Envora Flexi sheets are Class A non-combustible, UV stable (Grade 5 certified non-yellowing), hydrophobic with <0.5% water absorption, and freeze-thaw resistant from -40°C to +80°C.
