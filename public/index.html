Contractor FAQ

Installation & FAQs

Clear, practical answers for architects, interior designers, and installation contractors.

Flexible polymer tile adhesive (C2TE S1/S2 grade) is recommended for cement, plaster, and masonry substrates. For drywall, plywood, or tile-over-tile renovation, use high-strength MS polymer mastic or polyurethane structural adhesive with 100% notched troweled coverage.
Yes. Envora Flexible Stone cold-bends smoothly around cylindrical pillars, curved walls, and architectural arches down to a 200 mm radius without cracking or requiring heat-gun manipulation.
Sheets can be trimmed and cut cleanly on site using a standard heavy-duty utility knife, straight-edge ruler, or handheld shears. No wet diamond table saws, noisy machinery, or hazardous silica dust are produced.
Envora Flexible Stone is formulated with UV-stable mineral pigments and modified clay polymers. It is Class A fire rated, water-resistant, frost-thaw proof, and tested to withstand high exterior weather cycles.
Traditional natural marble slabs weigh 45 to 65 kg per square meter, requiring structural anchoring. Envora Flexible Stone weighs only ~3.5 to 5.5 kg per square meter—reducing dead load by approximately 85%.
