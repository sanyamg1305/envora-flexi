2. Product Character & Natural Variance

Envora Flexi produces Flexible Stone Sheets utilizing Modified Clay Material (MCM) engineered with natural stone mineral powder, modified clay, and polymer binders to recreate the texture, tone, and depth of real natural stone.

  • Natural Grain & Vein Variance: Natural stone designs, marble finishes, and textured collections exhibit deliberate, authentic tonal nuances and vein patterns. Samples provided serve as an aesthetic indicator of color palette and tactile grain; individual production lots may have natural variations inherent to mineral stone manufacturing.
  • Color Fastness & Batch Control: To ensure uniform consistency across large architectural elevations, clients are advised to order the complete square footage required for an elevation within a single production lot.

3. Technical Specifications & Thickness Profiles

Envora Flexi manufactures Flexible Stone Sheets in thicknesses ranging from 3 mm to 8 mm:

  • 3 mm Profile: Engineered for high-flexibility applications, interior curved feature walls, cylindrical columns, and architectural arches down to a minimum cold-bend radius of $R \ge 200\text{ mm}$ under normal ambient installation temperatures.
  • 5 mm Profile: All-round profile balancing dimensional stability with moderate curvature capability for interior living areas, lobbies, and gentle wall transitions.
  • 8 mm Profile: Stiffened architectural cladding designed for large planar exterior facades, high-traffic commercial zones, and heavy structural wear requirements.

Curvature Advisory: Bending radius depends on sheet thickness, profile grain direction, and ambient temperature (recommended $\ge 15^\circ\text{C}$). Never force sheets beyond their specified structural bend radius or apply reverse creasing.

5. Dispatch, Logistics & Transit

All commercial consignments are packaged on rigid architectural crates or reinforced protective pallets to safeguard the flexible sheets during transit. Delivery is made to the curbside or site unloading point indicated in the purchase order. The Purchaser is responsible for material offloading and site storage in a dry, shaded area protected from standing water and direct rain prior to installation.

6. Inspection, Acceptance & Claim Protocols

Upon receipt of consignments at the project site:

  • The Client or their appointed project supervisor must inspect the physical shipment for transit damage before signing the consignment note.
  • Sheets must be dry-laid and visually verified for finish, dimension, and color alignment prior to cutting, trimming, or adhesive application.
  • Pre-Installation Claim Rule: In accordance with global stone cladding trade conventions, once material has been adhered, cut, or bonded to a wall substrate, it is deemed accepted by the Purchaser, and no claims for visible surface variance or dimension can be entertained.

Envora Flexi is solely a material manufacturer and supplier. Installation must be conducted by qualified contractors adhering strictly to our technical installation guidelines:
